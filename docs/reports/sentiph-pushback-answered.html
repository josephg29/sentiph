Challenge 2 — the “chained to a moving target”

Fair — and you caught a real contradiction

You spotted the hole: I said “use their stuff and you're chained” in one breath and “use their stuff and you're free” in the next. Both can't be true. The thing that decides which one you get is how you touch their stuff:

Front door · NOT chained

Ask their tool to do the job

You don't copy anything. You run their command / call their official tool and take the result. When they upgrade the engine behind the door, the door stays the same and your result just gets better. You maintain nothing of theirs — only your own thin layer that asks.

Backyard copy · CHAINED

Reproduce how it works inside

You copy their internal machinery as it works today and bake it into Sentiph. But they keep changing theirs, and your copy is frozen at the moment you took it. So you either fall behind, or re-copy forever — and every bug in the copy is now yours.

The one-liner: plug into the power grid and the utility upgrades the plant for you — you just plug in. Build a copy of their power plant in your backyard and you're the one fixing it at 3 a.m. every time they improve theirs. “Chained” only happens with the backyard copy. The front door is free.

And honestly — if by “copying” you just meant “use the official thing Claude already ships,” then copying was never the wrong answer. That's the front door. That is the recommendation. The only thing to avoid is copying the unsupported internals — the part with no stable door, that changes without warning.

🔎 A real example you already live with
